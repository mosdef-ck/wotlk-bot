# Product Overview

MosdefAIBot is a World of Warcraft addon for Wrath of the Lich King (3.3.0) that provides AI-driven automation for raid encounters. The addon enables automated character control including DPS rotations, healing, movement, and boss-specific mechanics handling.

## Core Functionality

- Automated combat rotations for multiple classes (Mage, Warlock, Shaman, Priest, Paladin, Druid)
- Boss encounter modules for major raids (ICC, Ulduar, Naxxramas, ToGC, VoA, EoE)
- Pathfinding and automated movement
- Raid coordination through addon messaging
- Zone-aware behavior switching
- Configurable team composition and targeting priorities

## Target Users

Players running multiple WoW clients simultaneously who need automated assistance for raid encounters, particularly for managing alt characters or multi-boxing scenarios.
